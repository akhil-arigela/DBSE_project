THE PROBLEM
Fragmented data slows real estate search
Redundant, unnormalized data
Listing sites often duplicate property, agent, and media data across tables, wasting storage and risking inconsistency.
Slow, inconsistent search results
Without proper indexing and schema design, filtered searches by location, price, or type degrade as the catalogue grows.
No support for rich media
Photos and 360┬░ virtual tour files are rarely modeled cleanly alongside relational listing data.
Poor concurrency handling
Simultaneous bookings, edits, and searches from many users can overload a single, non-distributed backend.
